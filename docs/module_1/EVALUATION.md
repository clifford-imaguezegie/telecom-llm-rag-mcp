# Module 1 Evaluation

## Systems

- General LLM: `EssentialAI/rnj-1-instruct`
- Telecom LLM: `farbodtavakkoli/OTel-LLM-8.3B-IT`
- Independent Track 1 judge: `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct`

## Benchmarks

### Track 1
20 custom telecom engineering questions covering conceptual, procedural, troubleshooting, design and applied engineering tasks.

### Track 2
32 selected industry benchmark questions across eight telecom benchmark families.

## Final expert-reviewed results

| Metric | General LLM | OTel 1.0 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Track 1 Questions Won | **13 / 20 (65%)** | 7 / 20 (35%) |
| Track 1 Average Expert Score | **72.8 / 100** | 60.1 / 100 |
| Track 2 Questions Won | **23 / 32 (71.9%)** | 7 / 32 (21.9%) |
| Track 2 Ties | 2 | 2 |
| Track 2 Average Expert Score | **70.1 / 100** | 32.1 / 100 |
| Combined Questions Won | **36 / 52 (69.2%)** | 14 / 52 (26.9%) |
| Combined Average Expert Score | **71.1 / 100** | 42.8 / 100 |

## Main conclusion

> Technical fluency and concise answers do not guarantee technical correctness.

The General LLM was stronger overall, particularly on applied engineering, cloud-native telecom, complex architecture and troubleshooting. OTel 1.0 remained competitive on selected focused telecom questions but showed more non-responsive behaviour on complex/context-dependent tasks.
